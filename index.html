Add statements
Not counted as spending: payments on the cards, payments to cards from your bank, transfers, and money coming in. Returns aren't subtracted from spending. They're listed separately on their day and in the box under the calendar. You can tap any item to count it or not.
Where to download: on each card's website, open the account's transactions and look for Download, then choose CSV. Kohl's, Venture and Savor are all on capitalone.com.

Saved months

A snapshot keeps a finished month's daily totals and purchases after you clear the transactions. Saved months show on the calendar when you go back to them. They're kept in this browser, so for a permanent copy, also save the month's report.

Monthly bills

Card bills: tap ↻ Monthly on any charge and it's remembered. Each month it shows up grayed out on its day until the real charge arrives on a statement. Bank bills you add below are counted automatically on their day. All of these stay saved when you clear transactions.

Add a bill you pay from the bank Mortgage, utilities, insurance and the like. No statement needed: it's counted on its day each month.
Back up or restore your bills, cards and saved months

Your bills, card list and saved months are kept in this browser only. Copy the backup and keep it somewhere safe, like a note on your phone.
